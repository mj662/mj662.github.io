Best Clock Radio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

Best Clock Radio and Beautiful Clock Radio do not require an account, display third-party ads, sell personal information, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

This Privacy Policy explains how Mark Linthicum, the developer of the apps and operator of Enjoyable Apps ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information in connection with:

  • Best Clock Radio, including its free and paid App Store versions; and
  • Beautiful Clock Radio Alarm.

We refer to them together as the "Apps." This policy also describes limited data handling on the BeautifulClockRadio.com website.

Information handled by the Apps

The information involved depends on the features you choose to use. The Apps have no user-account system and do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, or advertising identifier.

Location for weather

If you grant location permission, the Apps use your device's location while the App is in use to request local weather and, in Beautiful Clock Radio, to select time- and weather-aware backgrounds. Coordinates are sent to AccuWeather to locate the relevant forecast. The most recently resolved location and weather are cached on your device so weather can remain useful between updates. We do not use location for advertising or to track your movements.

Apple Music and media-library access

If you grant media-library permission, the Apps read the music, playlists, and related metadata needed to let you choose and play them. Your selections and programmable-button settings are stored on your device. We do not upload a copy of your music library to our servers. Apple may process requests made through Apple Music and StoreKit under Apple's own privacy terms.

Alarms, stations, podcasts, and preferences

Alarm times, sound choices, saved station or podcast details, playback progress, weather preferences, and other settings are primarily stored locally on your device. They remain there until you change or reset them or delete the App, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

Internet radio, podcasts, searches, and links

When you search for or play online content, the App connects to the applicable directory, feed, station, podcast, or media host. Those providers necessarily receive the request, your IP address, and ordinary connection information and can learn which stream, feed, or page was requested. Search text is sent to the selected directory provider. These independent providers control their own data practices.

Diagnostics and station-repair information

Best Clock Radio uses Firebase Crashlytics to help us find and fix crashes and non-fatal errors. Reports can include a random app installation identifier, app version, device model, operating system, timestamps, stack traces, application state, and custom diagnostic context such as a failed station name or URL, playback error, podcast identifiers and progress, or alarm-screen state. We do not add your name, email address, or Apple ID to these reports.

When a catalog radio station fails, either App may automatically send a station-repair report to our service. It can include the station name and URL, error details, whether playback was for an alarm, app version and build, bundle identifier, and the status or expiration date of the feature entitlement used for automatic station updates. Reports may be forwarded to our private support workspace so we can repair the station. The report does not include your name or contact information.

Purchases and entitlements

Apple processes App Store purchases, subscriptions, payments, refunds, and Family Sharing. The Apps receive product and transaction status needed to unlock features and restore purchases; they do not receive your full payment-card number. Verified entitlement details may be cached securely on your device.

When Beautiful Clock Radio authorizes a purchased Photo or PhotoSet download, it sends an Apple-signed transaction, product identifier, PhotoSet identifier, and device class (iPhone or iPad) to our Cloudflare-hosted authorization service. The signed transaction is used to verify ownership and is not saved in our application database. Service logs may record the product, PhotoSet, device class, transaction identifier, time, IP address, and ordinary request information for security and troubleshooting.

Support communications

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use that information only to answer you, provide support, and improve the Apps.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide weather, streaming, podcast, alarm, and media features;
  • save your choices and verify access to purchased features;
  • diagnose crashes, repair broken stations, and improve reliability;
  • secure our services, prevent fraud or abuse, and enforce purchase rights;
  • respond to support requests; and
  • comply with law and protect users, our rights, and our services.

We do not use this information for third-party advertising, create advertising profiles, sell personal information, or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Service providers and other third parties

We use a limited number of providers to operate App features. Where a provider processes information for us, we require it to protect that information consistently with this policy and applicable law and to use it only to provide the contracted service. Providers include:

  • Apple for the App Store, StoreKit, purchases, subscriptions, Apple Music, and iOS permissions;
  • AccuWeather for location lookup and weather data;
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud for Best Clock Radio crash diagnostics and for the station-repair service used by the Apps;
  • Cloudflare for Beautiful Clock Radio's optional Photo and PhotoSet catalog, purchase authorization, and downloads;
  • Slack for private operational station-repair alerts; and
  • radio stations, podcast publishers, feed and search directories, and media hosts chosen through the Apps, such as TuneIn, SHOUTcast, Apple, and the operator of a custom URL you enter.

Independent content providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control the content, availability, or privacy practices of an independent station, publisher, website, or media host.

The website

BeautifulClockRadio.com is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub and its infrastructure providers receive IP addresses and browser/request information needed to deliver and secure the site. The home page also loads resources from Google Fonts and Tailwind's content-delivery network and contains an embedded YouTube video. Google/YouTube and those network providers may receive connection information and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. We do not receive a list identifying who visits the site and do not place our own advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

  • Information stored only on your device remains until you change or reset it, clear the relevant data, or delete the App, subject to iOS backups and Keychain behavior.
  • Google states that Firebase Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning deletion from live and backup systems.
  • Station-repair records and operational alerts are retained while needed to diagnose and repair a station, recognize repeat failures, secure the service, and document the resolution. They are then deleted or reduced to non-identifying aggregate or station records when no longer reasonably necessary.
  • Purchase-authorization data is processed for verification and security. We do not maintain user profiles from purchase data. Providers may retain security and request logs under their own retention schedules.
  • Support messages are retained while needed to answer the request, maintain a reasonable support history, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations, and are then deleted when no longer needed.

We may retain information longer when required by law, needed for a legal claim, or necessary to prevent fraud or protect the security of users and services.

Your choices and rights

  • You can allow or revoke Location and Media & Apple Music access in iOS Settings. Revoking access disables the features that need it.
  • You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings. Notifications are important to the Apps' backup-alarm behavior.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • You can remove locally stored App data by resetting applicable settings or deleting the App, subject to iOS backups and Keychain behavior.
  • You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information we control, or to object to or withdraw consent for processing where applicable.

Because the Apps do not use accounts and most diagnostics are identified only by random technical identifiers, we may not be able to connect a particular record to you without additional details. We will not ask for more information than reasonably necessary to verify and fulfill a request. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to appeal our response or complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases for processing

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we process information as needed to provide features you request and perform our agreement with you; with your consent for permission-based access such as location and media; for our legitimate interests in reliability, support, security, and fraud prevention where those interests are not overridden by your rights; and to comply with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

California and other U.S. state disclosures

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use or disclose precise location to infer characteristics about you; it is used only to provide the weather feature you request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right. You may use the contact information below to submit a request or authorize an agent where permitted by law.

Children's privacy

The Apps are general-audience utilities and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information involved, including Apple's permission system, signed purchase verification, encrypted HTTPS connections for our operated services, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms and provider safeguards for international transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when App features, providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will provide additional notice when required.

Contact

The data controller for information covered by this policy is Mark Linthicum / Enjoyable Apps. For privacy questions or requests, email enjoyableapps@gmail.com.
