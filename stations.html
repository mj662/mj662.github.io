We’re requesting your permission to include your station in Enjoyable Apps radio players, including Best Clock Radio, Beautiful Clock Radio and future radio-player apps.

Our fixed, non-scrolling preset buttons stay in predictable positions. They help anyone find saved stations, especially older listeners and people with vision or other accessibility challenges.

Radio listening, alarms and sleep timer use are free. Users pay only if they choose to activate weather in either clock radio app or purchase background images in Beautiful Clock Radio.

Please submit the form if you’re authorized to grant permission for your station. We’ll review the details before adding or updating a listing.

Permission wording

I am authorized to grant these permissions on behalf of this station. I give Enjoyable Apps permission to list this station, provide playback of its official live stream, and display station artwork that we supply or approve in its current and future radio-player apps, including Best Clock Radio and Beautiful Clock Radio. This includes live listening and, where available, radio alarms and sleep timer use, subject to the station’s geographic restrictions and any conditions entered above.

Permission version: 2026-10-27. Artwork is optional and may be used in future apps or features. Best Clock Radio and Beautiful Clock Radio currently do not display station logos.

Your contact details and permission response are kept private. The form is provided through Google Forms. Read how we handle station submissions.
