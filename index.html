More ways to listen

Radio, podcasts, and your music.

Best Clock Radio and Beautiful Clock Radio bring your everyday listening together with familiar, programmable favorites.

Apple Music

Listen to songs, albums, and playlists on Apple Music using the apps’ music integration. With your permission, access your music library and choose music you enjoy.

An active Apple Music subscription is required for Apple Music catalog playback. Apple Music is a separate service; membership is not included with either clock radio app or its in-app purchases. Availability varies by country or region.

Podcasts

Keep favorite shows on programmable buttons, browse their episodes, and use playback controls to pause or jump backward and forward. Go from your clock to your next episode without switching between station apps.

Podcasts use the apps’ podcast player. An Apple Music subscription is not required for podcast playback.

Another way to make it yours

Beautiful Clock Radio

Your favorite sounds. A view that changes with your day.

Enjoy radio, podcasts, music, alarms, and a sleep timer with living photographic backgrounds. Optional PhotoSets transition from day to night and respond to the weather.

Make each favorite button your own: press and hold to program a station, podcast, music, or soothing sound, then tap to play. Beautiful Clock Radio has six programmable buttons on iPhone and eight on iPad.

Free download with optional in-app purchases. PhotoSets are sold separately. Live weather and automatic station updates are included for the first six months; continued access requires an optional subscription. Core clock radio features do not require a subscription.
